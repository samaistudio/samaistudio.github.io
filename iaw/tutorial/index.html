IsAIWorthIt in nine screens

A tool that shows a small business what its AI tools save and what the licences cost. Read from top to bottom. All businesses are fictional and all numbers are simulated.

  1. 1 of 9

    The answer in one line

    Steps 1 to 4 show Becker & Partner, a fictional tax office in Cologne with 11 people. The headline is the value of the time saved, minus what the AI licences cost. Under it: hours saved, how many people use AI, and how much goes to seats nobody uses.

  2. 2 of 9

    What to do next

    Every month ends with two or three concrete steps, such as cancelling unused seats. The links under them jump to the part of the page that answers each question.

  3. 3 of 9

    Month by month against the licences

    Bars show the value of the time saved. The dashed line is the licence cost. The light part of a bar only covers the licences; the dark part is the gain.

  4. 4 of 9

    Seats paid for but not used

    Each square is one licence seat: filled when someone used it, empty when nobody did. Becker & Partner pays for 5 seats nobody used. Teams appear only when they have 5 or more people, so the four tax advisors are not shown. With only one team hidden, its hours can be worked out from the company total, and the page says so.

  5. 5 of 9

    A business where AI does not pay off yet

    The hotel: a second fictional business. It pays €384 a month for licences and saves time worth €259. The tool says so plainly and names the steps that would change it.

  6. 6 of 9

    What an employee sees

    Back to Becker & Partner. Switch “View as” to Everyone. Costs, licences and single teams are removed from the data before it reaches this view, not just hidden. The value of the time saved stays visible.

  7. 7 of 9

    One month, two readers

    Each reader gets a separate report file (example for Everyone). Numbers for single people are never shown. Designed with § 87(1) No. 6 BetrVG and the EU AI Act in mind. Not legal advice; check with your works council.

  8. 8 of 9

    Also inside Odoo 18

    The same measurement as a native Odoo app with two access groups (Viewer and Manager), a usage import and a pivot per department. Departments under 5 people are combined into “Small teams (combined)”. Shown with test data for the fictional Rheinmark Handels GmbH in a local Odoo 18 Community.

  9. 9 of 9

    Export for AI

    Copy the report as plain text for ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot, or download it. Only what the current view shows is exported. A read-only MCP server gives AI assistants the same data.
