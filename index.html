2 · Automate it in Odoo

InboxToOdoo

Invoices in. Checked bookings out.

Since 11/2026 · solo project · n8n · Odoo 18 Community · JSON-RPC · XRechnung / ZUGFeRD

Checks every invoice against order and delivery (3-way match), VAT ID and IBAN; stops on a changed bank account; books only after a person clicks.

My part: the process design (which checks run, when it stops, what a person approves), the rules and tolerances, the Odoo demo company, 19 test cases, and the approval mails and forms.

Demo · Rheinmark Handels GmbH and its suppliers are made up

The process Mailbox → read → check against order and delivery → stop or ask → a person approves → Odoo bill
